# Market Lens

Use this lens for market opportunity, industry structure, scenario, and commercial prioritization research.

## Minimum Questions

1. What is inside and outside the market definition?
2. Which customer, workflow, geography, and time horizon matter?
3. What evidence exists for size, growth, demand, adoption friction, and regulation?
4. Who captures value and where do economics or bottlenecks sit?
5. Which scenarios or risks change the recommendation?

## Framework Discipline

Use market sizing, industry structure, PESTLE, Five Forces, or risk analysis only when the evidence and decision need them. Do not fill every framework section as a template exercise.

## Weak Data Handling

- State the sizing method and assumptions.
- Separate hard numbers from directional evidence.
- Use ranges when public metrics disagree.
- Make missing private-market or customer-economics data explicit.
