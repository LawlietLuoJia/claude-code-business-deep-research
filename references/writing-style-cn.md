# Chinese Commercial Writing Style

Use this reference when the report is written in Chinese. It improves readability without weakening evidence discipline.

## Rules

- Start with the decision answer, not background exposition.
- Prefer concrete business nouns and verbs. Avoid generic phrases such as `赋能`, `抓手`, `在当今时代`, and empty ranking phrases like `首先/其次/最后` when they only pad structure.
- Each major paragraph should carry one decision-relevant point: fact pattern, implication, risk, option, or action.
- Keep `Fact`, `Inference`, and `Gap` visible where they protect auditability.
- Make priority, tradeoff, and risk language explicit: say what should be done first, what should wait, and what evidence could change the view.
- Do not use sharper prose to overstate weak evidence. If the evidence is weak, the sentence must stay weak.

## Before Delivery

Check that the Chinese report:

- reads like a commercial decision memo rather than a generic AI summary
- keeps evidence IDs, source trails, or gap labels attached to material claims
- does not add unsupported ROI, market-size, ranking, or adoption claims for narrative smoothness
