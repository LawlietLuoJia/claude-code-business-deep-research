# Research Synthesis Lens

Use this lens when the source material includes interviews, surveys, feedback, tickets, call notes, observation logs, or similar user evidence.

## Pattern

1. Preserve each observation and its source context.
2. Distinguish behavior, attitude, workaround, pain point, positive signal, and quote.
3. Group observations into themes; keep contradictions and outliers visible.
4. Assess frequency, severity, segment, and confidence.
5. Translate findings into opportunity areas and follow-up questions.

## Evidence Boundary

Qualitative findings become stronger through triangulation across participants, methods, time, and quantitative data. A vivid quote is evidence, not a market-size estimate or final recommendation by itself.
