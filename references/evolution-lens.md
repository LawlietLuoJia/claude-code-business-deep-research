# Evolution Lens

Use this lens when history explains present commercial position.

## Questions

1. What problem, constraint, team, or external shift created the subject?
2. Which decisions changed its trajectory: product scope, distribution, pricing, financing, technology, partnerships, or regulation?
3. Which phases are distinct enough to matter?
4. What does the present competitive cross-section look like?
5. Which present advantages or liabilities are path-dependent?

## Output Pattern

- One compact timeline with evidence-backed turning points
- Current-position comparison against a small relevant peer or substitute set
- Cross insight: how past choices explain current opportunity, moat, weakness, or risk

Avoid chronology without causality. Historical color belongs only when it changes the judgment.
