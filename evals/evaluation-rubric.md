# Business Deep Research Evaluation Rubric

Use this rubric for no-skill, old-skill, and `business-deep-research` comparisons.

| Dimension | Weight | Passing signal |
|---|---:|---|
| Research framing | 15 | Identifies decision context, scope, questions, constraints, and a useful first gate |
| Source quality | 15 | Prefers primary or high-authority sources for core facts and distinguishes leads from evidence |
| Evidence traceability | 15 | Names the evidence artifacts and keeps material findings tied to sources |
| Uncertainty handling | 10 | Exposes contradictions, weak public data, gaps, assumptions, and inferences |
| Search accounting | 10 | Plans or records search route, failed/skipped searches, and fallback reasons |
| Business relevance | 15 | Converts research into decision-useful insights and implications |
| Framework discipline | 10 | Uses the minimum lens that fits the evidence and task |
| Readability | 5 | Produces a clear report shape rather than framework sludge |
| Interaction cost | 5 | Uses the two intended gates without turning every step into a questionnaire |
